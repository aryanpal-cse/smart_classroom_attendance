Timetable Rules

Each class section has its own weekly timetable. Students assigned to that section see the same subject, teacher, room, day and class-time information.

A teacher can teach several different sections during the same day. All those entries will appear together in that teacher's combined timetable.

The conflict checker prevents one teacher, one section or one room from being assigned to overlapping classes.

Timetable data is stored once in SQLite and reused by the Admin, Teacher and Student panels without creating duplicate schedule records.
